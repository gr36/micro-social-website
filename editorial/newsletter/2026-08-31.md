# Slow reads and a new challenge

Two books people can't put down, four accounts worth a follow, and the September photo challenge.

Hello, and welcome to the first Micro Wrapped. The idea is simple: once a week, a short note on what's happening around Micro.blog, a few things worth reading, and some picks. Nothing here is automated. If it's in this edition it's because someone on Micro.blog was talking about it.

## Interesting links

Three pages that explain more of Micro.blog than most people ever find. The first one is the reason the timeline looks the way it does.

- [Micro.blog and the open web](https://micro.blog/about/community) (Micro.blog). The community guidelines, and why the timeline has no likes, no follower counts and no algorithm.
- [How to run a photo challenge](https://micro.blog/challenges) (@challenges). Where the daily prompts come from, how to take part, and how the tag works.
- [Bookshelves on Micro.blog](https://help.micro.blog/t/bookshelves/234) (Help Center). Shelves, goals, and how the reading pages on your blog are built from them.

## Books

September is a reading month. Two novels keep coming up in the [books timeline](https://micro.blog/discover/books), one long and one you can finish in an evening. If you've not tried the Books tab yet, add one of them to Want to Read and see how the shelves work.

- [Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow](https://micro.blog/books/9780593321201) by Gabrielle Zevin. Read by a lot of people on Micro.blog this month
- [Small Things Like These](https://micro.blog/books/9781529032420) by Claire Keegan. A short one for a weekend
