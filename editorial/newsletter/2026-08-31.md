# Slow reads and a new challenge

Two books people can't put down, four accounts worth a follow, and the September photo challenge.

Hello, and welcome to the first Micro Wrapped. The idea is simple: once a week, a short note on what's happening around Micro.blog, a few things worth reading, and some picks. Nothing here is automated. If it's in this edition it's because someone on Micro.blog was talking about it.

September is a reading month. Two novels keep coming up in the [books timeline](https://micro.blog/discover/books), one long and one you can finish in an evening. If you've not tried the Books tab yet, add one of them to Want to Read and see how the shelves work.

The photo challenge is back too. The prompts come from [@challenges](https://micro.blog/challenges), one a day, and you take part by posting a photo with the day's tag. It's the easiest way to meet people here, so the account is this week's tip below.

If you're new, the four accounts further down are the ones to follow first. They'll tell you what's changing on the platform before anyone else does. See you next week.
